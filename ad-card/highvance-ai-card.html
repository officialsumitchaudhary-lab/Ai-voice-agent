AI Agent Card for the voice agent ad

A dark-blue, round-cornered call card that sits behind or above the presenter and shows the HighVance voice agent talking: a glowing agent orb, a live voice waveform and the agent's words appearing as it speaks. It runs as a 10-second loop so the editor can stretch it under any take.

  • Orb ring turns once per loop; three ripples leave the orb every 2.5 s and grow with the voice.
  • Waveform: 44 mirrored bars, louder in the middle, spreading outward from the centre on each word.
  • Words fade up one at a time; the newest word is ice blue, earlier words white.
