## CHUNK: The programme
- Master of Business Administration, offered online by NMIMS.
- Duration: two years, in four semesters.
- Built for working professionals, to manage studies alongside work and personal commitments.
- No career break needed: students keep working and can apply what they learn at work.

## CHUNK: How learning works
- Recorded lectures and live sessions.
- Live and interactive sessions are held in the evenings and on weekends. The exact schedule is in the academic calendar on the student's LMS account after enrolment.
- Doubt-clearing sessions, industry expert sessions, case-study discussions and masterclasses.
- Application-oriented assessments.
- Student portal, digital study material and the university's digital library.
- Faculty interaction happens through live sessions and doubt-clearing sessions.

## CHUNK: Exams
Exams are conducted online and are proctored.
Exam fee: eight hundred rupees per subject, for every exam applied for. Six subjects per semester, so four thousand eight hundred rupees per semester, and nineteen thousand two hundred rupees across four semesters when each exam is taken once.

## CHUNK: Specialisations
Seven specialisations: Marketing, Finance, Human Resources, Business Analytics, IT Management, Business Management, and Operations and Data Sciences.
The right one depends on the person's current role and career goal.

## CHUNK: Eligibility
- A bachelor's degree, ten plus two plus three, in any discipline, from a recognised university, or an equivalent degree recognised by the Association of Indian Universities, A I U.
- Minimum fifty percent at graduation. Forty five percent for SC, ST, OBC and PwD candidates.
- Any graduation stream is fine, including non-business degrees. Graduating many years ago is fine.
- No work experience required.
- Diploma plus degree holders are eligible. A diploma alone is not.
- Final-semester students can apply. Admission is confirmed after document submission and approval from the university.
- Below the required percentage, or unclear cases: the senior counsellor checks.

## CHUNK: Fees and payment options
Registration: eleven thousand two hundred rupees. That is an application fee of one thousand two hundred rupees, plus ten thousand rupees paid towards the tuition fee. The ten thousand is adjusted in every payment option, including EMI.
After registration, the student picks a payment option for the tuition fee:
- **One-time payment:** one lakh ninety six thousand rupees. Saves twenty four thousand rupees compared with paying per semester.
- **Annual:** one lakh five thousand rupees per year, two lakh ten thousand rupees in total. Saves ten thousand rupees compared with paying per semester.
- **Semester-wise:** fifty five thousand rupees per semester, two lakh twenty thousand rupees in total.
- **No-cost EMI:** eight thousand seven hundred fifty rupees per month for twenty four months, two lakh ten thousand rupees in total. Through a loan partner, subject to the applicant's approval. Any loan partner charges: the senior counsellor confirms.
No scholarship. Defence personnel get a fee concession; the senior counsellor gives details.

## CHUNK: Why an online MBA, general points
These are general points about online MBAs, not specific NMIMS claims.
- Flexibility: study from almost anywhere, balanced with a job and family.
- Career growth: skills for managerial and leadership roles, or moving into finance, marketing, operations, HR, consulting or strategy.
- Learn while you work: apply concepts at work, no career break.
- Lower overall cost: no relocation, commuting or hostel costs.
- Wider choice: universities outside your city without relocating.
- Digital and business skills: data analysis, presentations, project management, virtual teamwork.
- Networking: classmates, faculty, alumni and industry professionals, through virtual events, group projects and forums.

## CHUNK: Online or offline MBA
Neither is better for everyone; it depends on priorities.
- Offline suits someone who wants campus life, daily classroom learning, face-to-face networking, or to leave their job for a full-time MBA.
- Online suits someone who wants to keep working, needs flexibility, or can't attend college daily.
- NMIMS Online is more than recorded videos: live sessions, doubt-clearing, industry experts, case studies and masterclasses.
- Networking online works differently: industry expert sessions, masterclasses and the Alumni Portal.
- Online learning needs self-discipline; live and recorded sessions, the student portal and study resources help build a routine.
