## CHUNK: What Highvance is
Highvance is an AI voice and automation platform built for Indian businesses, at enterprise scale. It gives a company AI agents that make and take real phone calls, chat across messaging channels, and run the follow-up work after a conversation, all inside one platform with a built-in CRM.

## CHUNK: The company
Highvance Private Limited is an Indian registered company. Sumit Chaudhary is the founder, Prem Gupta is the co-founder, and Shreyank Saxena is the tech head.

## CHUNK: Who it is for
Businesses with more calling than people to make the calls. Highvance is built for enterprise-level volume and works across education, real estate, ecommerce, healthcare and financial services. Typical work it takes over: calling new leads the moment they come in, qualifying them, booking appointments, confirming orders and deliveries, reminders, and following up after a conversation.

## CHUNK: AI voice agents
The core product. Agents handle outbound and inbound calls, speak naturally, follow a conversation rather than a script, and do the job a calling team would do: qualify a lead, answer questions, book an appointment, confirm an order, give a reminder. Each agent is built for one business and one job, with its own knowledge base, so it is built to answer only from that company's real information.

## CHUNK: Hand-off to a human
Yes. An agent can hand a call over to a person on the business's own team when the conversation needs it.

## CHUNK: Languages
Agents speak English, Hindi, and natural Hinglish the way people actually talk on the phone. They also speak all Indian regional languages. An agent switches to the language the caller uses, mid-call, without being asked.

## CHUNK: Accents and noisy lines
Agents are built for Indian callers: Indian accents, mixed languages, and real phone-line conditions. How it performs for a specific customer base is something the team can show on a live demo.

## CHUNK: AI chatbot across channels
Beyond voice, the same AI works as a chatbot on WhatsApp, Instagram, email and a website widget: one assistant across the channels a customer already uses. Highvance is an approved WhatsApp Business partner.

## CHUNK: AI automation builder
An automation builder that works from plain language: describe the workflow you want and it builds it. This covers the work around a conversation: updating the CRM, tagging a lead, sending a message, booking into a calendar, triggering a follow-up.

## CHUNK: Integrations
Highvance integrates with all common business software, and builds custom integrations too. Whatever CRM or tool a business already uses, Highvance can connect to it. The team scopes the specific integration on a call.

## CHUNK: CRM and post-call automation
A CRM is built in, so calls, contacts, outcomes and follow-ups live in one place. After every call, the platform classifies the outcome, updates the contact record, applies tags, books appointments where relevant, and triggers whatever follow-up the business has set up. This happens natively, not through a third-party tool.

## CHUNK: Call records and analytics
Every call is logged with its recording and transcript, its outcome, and what the agent captured. Businesses see exactly what was said and what happened next, and track performance across campaigns.

## CHUNK: Scale and reliability
Highvance runs lakhs of calls, with telephony numbers across India. Capacity is planned around a client's volume rather than a fixed limit.

## CHUNK: Phone numbers
Whether a business can use its existing number, and for inbound or outbound, is answered by the team for that business's setup.

## CHUNK: Data and security
Customer data is stored in India, on servers in Mumbai, and is encrypted in transit and at rest. Specific vendors, architecture and model details are never discussed on a public channel.

## CHUNK: Compliance
Highvance calls run on TRAI compliant telephony. The platform has a built-in DND check, so numbers on the Do Not Disturb registry are screened, and calling follows customer consent.
Calls are recorded as each client requires, and recordings are deleted if the client asks.
Agents say they are AI whenever a caller asks, as compliance requires.
Anything more specific to a business's use case is answered by the team.

## CHUNK: Support
Support is at the heart of Highvance. Every client is supported fully, from setup to after go-live, and the team stays with them when anything needs fixing.

## CHUNK: How a business gets started
There is no self-serve signup and no free trial. A business contacts the team and gets a live demo. Then the use case is scoped and the agent is built for that business.
