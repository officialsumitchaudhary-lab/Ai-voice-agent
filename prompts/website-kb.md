## CHUNK: What Highvance is
Highvance is an AI voice and automation platform built for Indian businesses. It gives a company AI agents that make and take real phone calls, chat across messaging channels, and handle the follow-up work after a conversation, all in one platform with a built-in CRM. The company is Highvance Private Limited, registered in India.

## CHUNK: Who it is for
Businesses with more calling than people to do the calling. The platform is used across education, real estate, ecommerce, healthcare and financial services, from a single clinic to a large enterprise. Typical work it takes over: calling new leads the moment they arrive, qualifying them, booking appointments, confirming orders and deliveries, reminders, and the follow-up after a conversation.

## CHUNK: AI voice agents
The core product. Agents handle outbound and inbound calls, speak naturally, follow a conversation rather than a script, and do what a calling team would do: qualify a lead, answer questions, book an appointment, confirm an order, give a reminder. Each agent is built for one business and one job, with its own knowledge base, so it answers from that company's real information instead of guessing.

## CHUNK: Languages
Agents speak English, Hindi and natural Hinglish the way people actually talk on the phone, and support Indian regional languages. An agent switches to the language the caller uses, mid-call, without being asked.

## CHUNK: Built in-house
The speech recognition, the language model and the voice are Highvance's own, not borrowed from third-party providers. That is why the agents handle Indian speech, Hinglish and regional languages the way they do, and why the platform can be tuned for a client rather than taking whatever a vendor ships. Specific model names, architecture and internals are never discussed on a public channel. **[VERIFY: confirm whether the in-house model names may be used publicly before any of them is spoken.]**

## CHUNK: AI chatbot across channels
The same AI also works as a chatbot on WhatsApp, Instagram, email and a website widget: one assistant across the channels a customer already uses, rather than a separate tool for each.

## CHUNK: AI automation builder
An automation builder that works from plain language: describe the workflow you want and it builds it. This covers the work around a conversation: updating the CRM, tagging a lead, sending a message, booking into a calendar, triggering a follow-up.

## CHUNK: CRM and post-call automation
A CRM layer is built in, so calls, contacts, outcomes and follow-ups live in one place. After every call the platform classifies the outcome, updates the contact record, applies tags, books appointments where relevant, and triggers whatever follow-up the business has set up. This is native to the platform, not a third-party tool bolted on.

## CHUNK: Call records and analytics
Every call is logged with its recording and transcript, its outcome, and what the agent captured. Recordings are kept on Highvance's own platform. Businesses can see exactly what was said, what happened next, and how campaigns are performing.

## CHUNK: Scale
The platform is built for large calling volumes, lakhs of calls, with telephony numbers across India. Capacity is planned around a client's volume rather than a fixed limit.

## CHUNK: Security
Customer data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and the platform runs on enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure. Call recordings stay on Highvance's own platform. ## CHUNK: How a business gets started
There is no self-serve signup and no free trial. It starts with a conversation with the Highvance team: the use case gets scoped, and the agent is built for that business with its own knowledge base. Anyone who wants that conversation is pointed to the team. The agent does not arrange it, schedule it, or take details for it.

## CHUNK: Pricing
Calling costs five rupees per minute. The platform itself is free to use, so a business pays for the minutes it actually uses and nothing more.
For high volume there are volume discounts, and the team puts together a custom plan based on how much calling a business expects. Anyone with real volume is pointed to the team for that.
This is the whole of what the agent knows about price. The five-rupee rate is stated plainly whenever asked, and the volume discount is mentioned alongside it only when volume is relevant. Beyond this: no setup figure, no plan names, no discount percentages, no custom quote, no comparison with anyone else's pricing. For any of those, the honest answer is that the team works it out against actual volume.
Never invent a figure, never estimate a monthly total, never say "roughly" about a number not written here.
Spoken as words, with the currency word last: "five rupees per minute" in English, "paanch rupaye per minute" in Hinglish. Never a digit, never a rupee symbol.
