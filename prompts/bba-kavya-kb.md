## CHUNK: About the Academy
Be Better Academy is a coaching and academic support institute in Pune, with two branches: Dehuroad and Mukai Chowk.
It wants to be seen as a trusted academic and career-guidance partner for students and parents, never a discount-focused coaching centre.
Focus: strong fundamentals, concept clarity, regular testing, personal attention, discipline, student monitoring, parent communication, board preparation, competitive exam preparation and career guidance.
Approach: first understand the student's current academic position, goal and challenges, then recommend a programme.

## CHUNK: Boards and classes

## CHUNK: Classes four to ten
School subject coaching with concept clarity, regular practice, homework support, tests, exam preparation, doubt solving and academic monitoring.
Maharashtra State Board coaching adds board-oriented preparation.

## CHUNK: Class ten board preparation
Special board-focused preparation for Maharashtra State Board Class ten students.
Guidance on the student's path after Class ten: stream, college and competitive exam planning together.

## CHUNK: Class eleven integrated admission
The key campaign. For Class ten families, the Academy helps plan the move from Class ten to Class eleven and builds a structured path towards boards and the student's goal.
Never pressure a career choice. The counselling visit and demo exist to understand the student's strengths, interests and goals, then suggest a path.
Key message: after Class ten, stream, college and competitive exam planning should be considered together.

## CHUNK: Classes eleven and twelve, Science
Boards plus entrance exam preparation, depending on the student's subjects and goal: MHT CET, JEE Main and Advanced, NEET UG, NDA.
Integrated, so students don't need separate coaching for boards and each entrance exam.

## CHUNK: Classes eleven and twelve, Commerce
Boards plus CA Foundation preparation.
Other commerce entrance exams: the Academy team confirms.

## CHUNK: Free demo
