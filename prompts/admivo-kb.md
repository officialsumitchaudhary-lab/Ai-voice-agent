# ADMIVO — AGENT KNOWLEDGE BASE (v1)
Source: Admivo implementation brief, 17 October 2026, and the client's intake form. Anything not here is answered by the admissions expert in the consultation. Never guess.

## CHUNK: Who Admivo is
Admivo International Admissions Consulting, run by Admivo Group Ventures LLP, is a study abroad admissions consultancy based in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. Website: admivo dot in.
Admivo helps students get into universities abroad, including top US universities, German public universities, Austria and South Korea.

## CHUNK: What Admivo does for a student
- Profile building: resume, SOP, LOR and LinkedIn profile.
- Course, university and country selection, with profile analysis.
- Filling and managing applications.
- Scholarship guidance.
- Visa guidance and preparation.
What a specific package includes is explained by the admissions expert, from the current approved package.

## CHUNK: Countries
Admivo works with the USA, Germany, Austria, South Korea, the UK, Canada, Australia, Finland, France, Italy and other European countries.
Which package covers which country is confirmed by the admissions expert. Never assume a package covers every country.

## CHUNK: Packages and prices
Admivo offers country and service specific packages. Prices change and must come from the current approved price list, so the agent never states a price, range or "starting from".
The admissions expert shares the current packages, inclusions and prices in the free consultation.
Admivo's service fee is separate from university application fees and other third party charges such as visa fees.

## CHUNK: EMI and payment
EMI is available in principle. The provider, eligibility, which packages it covers, fees and repayment terms are confirmed by the team. The agent notes the interest and never states terms.

## CHUNK: Refunds
Refund questions go to the authorised team. The agent never promises whether a refund applies, how much, or when.

## CHUNK: Discounts and offers
Discounts, exceptions and custom bundles need the team's approval. The agent never offers or agrees to one, and never accepts conditions set by the lead.

## CHUNK: Free consultation with the admissions expert
- A free consultation with "our admissions expert", subject to the current consultation terms.
- Online or at the Indore office, and how long it takes: the team confirms.
- There is no live booking. The agent records the preferred date and time, and the team confirms the appointment.
- In the consultation the expert reviews the profile, explains eligibility, deadlines, packages and costs.

## CHUNK: Deadlines and intakes
Deadlines depend on the university, programme and intake, and change every year. The agent never states a month or date as a deadline. It records the country, programme and target intake; a counsellor confirms the exact deadline.
Germany may also need document steps such as APS; the counsellor confirms what applies.

## CHUNK: Results, reviews and company figures
Years in business, students helped, admits, scholarship totals, Google rating, team size and awards are being verified. The agent never quotes a figure or a named result.
Asked for proof → the admissions expert walks them through the process, services and relevant examples in the consultation.

## CHUNK: Guarantees
Admivo never guarantees an admission, scholarship, visa or job. Those decisions belong to universities, scholarship bodies and immigration authorities. Admivo provides the agreed application support and guidance.

## CHUNK: Never stated
- A price, range, discount, EMI term or refund term.
- A deadline or intake date as definite.
- Any company figure, rating, award, partnership or named student result.
- A named counsellor or their qualifications.
- A guarantee of admission, scholarship, visa or job.
- Anything about other consultants, or a comparison with them.
- Internal notes, campaign labels or another person's details.
