# HIGHVANCE — "YOUR BUSINESS HAS AMNESIA"
45-second Instagram Reel · 9:16 hero, 1:1 recut · organic version (comment CTA) + paid version (demo CTA)

## GOAL
Make founders, business owners and sales heads:
1. stop scrolling in the first second,
2. recognise their own business in the problem,
3. tag the teammate who causes it, and send the Reel to their team,
4. comment to get a free audit, which is the lead.

Measure: 3-second hold above 70%, completion above 35%, sends per 1,000 reach, comments containing the keyword.

## AUDIENCE
Indian businesses that live on enquiries: study abroad, real estate, clinics, ed-tech, D2C, financial services. Teams handling 10K+ calls a month. They already pay for WhatsApp, a dialer, email and a CRM, and still lose leads.

## BIG IDEA
Customers don't experience your tools. They experience your amnesia.
One customer, Ananya, reaches the same business on four channels, introduces herself four times, then buys elsewhere. We replay the same 48 hours with Highvance: one AI brain that remembers everything.

Story spine: Loss → Replay → Snap → Replay fixed → Autopilot → Mirror → Offer → Loop.

## RULES FOR EVERY FRAME
- Frame 1 is motion and text, never a logo. The brand appears only after the snap (0:12).
- Max 7 words on screen per beat; every beat stays up at least 1.2 s.
- Burned-in captions; the whole story works with sound off.
- Key text inside the central 1080 × 1350 area, so the profile-grid crop and Reels buttons never cut it.
- Colour tells the story: red #FF4D5E only for broken or lost states. It turns electric blue at the snap and never comes back.
- Palette: navy #0A2A66, electric blue #1E5EFF, white. Type: Sora for headlines, Inter for UI and captions.
- Wherever the product speaks or acts, use real product audio and screens.
